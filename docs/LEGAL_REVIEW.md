# Legal and privacy launch decisions

The app publishes `/privacy` and `/terms` so the product has reviewable public surfaces. They intentionally display a pre-launch warning and do not invent facts that only the owner or qualified counsel can decide.

Before accepting public registrations or payments, the owner and Indian counsel must provide or approve:

- legal entity name, entity type, registered address and contact details;
- grievance officer/contact and the final DPDP request and appeal process;
- lawful-basis and consent language for customer/supplier data and optional WhatsApp/email messages;
- retention periods for invoices, tax records, audit logs, deleted accounts, backups and provider logs;
- cross-border processing disclosures and data-processing terms for Neon, Railway, Vercel, Razorpay, email, Meta, storage, monitoring and the selected GSP/IRP provider;
- renewal, cancellation, refund, failed-payment and downgrade terms;
- warranty, liability cap, indemnity, termination, governing-law and dispute clauses;
- age/authority requirements and whether any consumer-facing use is permitted;
- the effective date and a process for notifying users of material changes.

The current page copy is product documentation, not legal advice or a claim of DPDP compliance. Remove the visible draft warning only after counsel and the legal operator approve the final text.
